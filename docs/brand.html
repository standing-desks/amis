Color and use

Cyan #62eaff and pink #ff4fd8 form the weave. Green #8cce42 and #b9ff5b mark the leaf. Charcoal #202326 keeps the small icon legible on light and dark surfaces. Keep the mark's proportions, use at least 24 pixels for the full mark, and allow clear space equal to one strand width.
